## What this file is for

`audience.md` is your account's **audience persona** — "who is watching / who will leave a comment / who will repost".

It is parallel to `rubric_notes.md`: both are derived from retro data.

```
Retro data (comments + completion + follows)
   ├──→ rubric evolution (cheat-bump)   —— "how to score"
   └──→ audience persona (cheat-persona) —— "who is watching"
```

cheat-seed reads it when choosing topics / writing scripts, adding one more mirror for "would this persona care?".

**It is not hand-written** — `/cheat-persona` derives it by clustering the comments in the retrospective sections of `predictions/*.md`. A hand-written persona is your fantasy; a data-derived persona is your real audience.
